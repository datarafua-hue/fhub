# Hidden Hinges for Interior Doors

Hidden hinges are a modern solution for interior doors that provides an elegant appearance and functionality.

## Advantages of Hidden Hinges

1. **Aesthetics** — hinges are completely hidden, the door looks seamless
2. **Reliability** — can withstand heavy loads
3. **Durability** — last for decades with proper installation

## Installation

The installation process requires precision and attention to detail. It's important to correctly mark the locations for hinge mortising.
